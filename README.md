Many if not all mod and stock planes in NO have very basic flight control laws, with simple pitch/roll/yaw command and AoA G limit. 
This mod is specifically made for Aryx’s F-22E mod to replace the FCS and with some minor CoM and Aerodynamics tweaks. 
Link to Aryx’s F-22E: https://github.com/Aryx3D/Aryx_F22E_StrikeRaptor

This mod replaces the basic FCS with AoA command, G command, roll/yaw blending at high AoA, and carefree handling across the flight regime. 

Overview list of control laws:

 -AoA cmd: mapping AoA request directly to the pilot’s stick position at low airspeed, the FCS will calculate the pitch rate needed to reach and sustain the pilot commanded AoA within the available pitch rate budget.
	+Stick position mapped to AoA request.
	+Release stick defaults to hold the plane at zero pitch rate within the current AoA limit.
	+To prevent easing stick input causing the nose to shove down, the plane will decelerate the pitch rate which floors at zero to the next lower AoA request.
	+Kinematic braking, the FCS knows the current effectiveness of control surfaces, torque generated by TVC, aircraft weight to calculate how much pitch authority the plane has and dynamically slows down the pitch rate right before hitting AoA cmd from the pilot to prevent overshot.

-G cmd: mapping G request directly to the pilot’s stick position at high airspeed, the FCS will calculate the pitch rate needed to reach and sustain the pilot commanded G within the available pitch rate budget.
	+Stick position mapped to G request.
	+Release stick defaults to hold the plane at zero pitch rate within the current G limit.
	+To prevent easing stick input causing the nose to shove down, the plane will decelerate the pitch rate which floors at zero to the next lower G request.

- AoA and G cmd are blended as airspeed increases.

-Roll/Yaw: pure rate command, as AoA increases, roll will introduce more yaw into the same direction.
	+Past 40 degrees of AoA, roll and yaw input starts to blend and become unified as the same control axis. 

-Flaps: reduced scheduling based on airspeed, dynamically deployed to minimize AoA and disengage as aoa increases.

*Rolling on flaperons are prioritized over flaps to maximize roll authority at low airspeed.

-Pitch rate cmd: only available with landing gears are down or stability assist is off. With stability assist off, AoA limit is removed and max G increased from +9.5/-3.5 to +12.5/-3.5.

-Pitch rate limiter: maximum budget is 48-50deg/s, dynamically reducing based on current effectiveness of control surfaces, airspeed, torque generated by TVC, aircraft weight to estimate the current available pitch authority.

-Roll rate limiter: maximum budget is around 250deg/s, dynamically reducing based on current effectiveness of control surfaces, airspeed, AoA, aircraft weight to estimate the current available roll authority, with braking margin increases as airspeed reduces.

- Yaw rate limiter: : maximum budget is around 75deg/s, dynamically reducing based on current effectiveness of control surfaces, airspeed, AoA, aircraft weight to estimate the current available yaw authority, with braking margin increases as airspeed reduces.

*Both yaw and roll cannot exceed each other’s rate.

-Envelope protection override: Automatically shove the nose to prevent the plane from exceeding AoA limit. Can be overridden by full stick opposing the pitch recovery direction to maintain zero pitch rate.
